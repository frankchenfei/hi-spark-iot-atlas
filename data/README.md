## What is not included

- Brand and platform logos (third-party trademarks, used on the site under their owners terms).
- Marketplace ranks, prices and ratings: sample data on the site until a licensed source is connected.
- Personal data, analytics and community contributions.

## Accuracy

Every record carries a lastVerified date and links to an official source. Requirements change: confirm the current version with the issuing body or a test laboratory before making a compliance decision.
