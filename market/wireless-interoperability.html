What to prepare

  • Choose the certifications that match the marketing claims
  • Test at an authorised laboratory using the correct test plan
  • Transfer the module certification conditions to the host design
  • Display the certification marks and identifiers correctly
  • Join the Bluetooth SIG as a member
  • Use qualified components and record their declarations
  • Complete the declaration and product listing
  • Keep the qualified design version aligned with shipped firmware

Frequently asked questions

Which certifications are mandatory to sell an IoT device in Wireless and interoperability?

Wi-Fi CERTIFIED (Wi-Fi Alliance certification), Bluetooth SIG (Bluetooth qualification). Each record lists the issuing body, what it covers and what it does not cover.

Are voluntary marks needed as well?

Wi-Fi CERTIFIED - Wi-Fi Alliance certification; Matter - Matter certification; Thread - Thread certification; Zigbee - Zigbee certification; Qi - Qi wireless charging certification

Where can I read the official requirement?

Every certification record links to the issuing body or the regulation text. Start from this page, open the record and follow its source links.

Does a certificate for a module cover the finished product?

No. A module certificate covers the module; the finished device usually needs its own approval or a documented module grant reference. Confirm with the issuing body or your test laboratory.
