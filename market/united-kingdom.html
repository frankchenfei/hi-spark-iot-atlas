IoT certification requirements in United Kingdom

Great Britain runs its own UKCA framework alongside continued CE recognition for most goods.

5 certification records apply · last reviewed 2026-10-14

Certifications that apply

UKCA

UKCA marking (Great Britain)

The UK conformity assessment marking used for most products placed on the market in Great Britain, with separate rules in Northern Ireland.

What to prepare

  • Confirm whether CE or UKCA is the accepted route for your product
  • Use a UK approved body where third-party assessment is required
  • Keep the UK declaration of conformity with UK responsible person details
  • Apply the UKCA mark and correct labelling
  • Register in every EU country where you place goods
  • Report put-on-market volumes on time
  • Print the crossed-out bin symbol and producer identification
  • Contract with a take-back scheme where required

Frequently asked questions

Which certifications are mandatory to sell an IoT device in United Kingdom?

UKCA (UKCA marking (Great Britain)), WEEE (Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment), RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances), EMC (EMC Directive 2014/30/EU). Each record lists the issuing body, what it covers and what it does not cover.

Are voluntary marks needed as well?

EN 303 645 - ETSI EN 303 645 consumer IoT security baseline

Where can I read the official requirement?

Every certification record links to the issuing body or the regulation text. Start from this page, open the record and follow its source links.

Does a certificate for a module cover the finished product?

No. A module certificate covers the module; the finished device usually needs its own approval or a documented module grant reference. Confirm with the issuing body or your test laboratory.
